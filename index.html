Frequently asked questions

1. What is the Tanzania STG app?

It is a convenient clinical reference app designed to help healthcare professionals consult Tanzania’s Standard Treatment Guidelines (STG).

2. What information can I find in the app?

The app provides access to STG clinical reference content, with chapters and sections for looking up treatment recommendations. It is intended to make guideline information easier to consult when needed.

3. Does the app work without internet?

Yes. The reference content is stored in the app, so you can consult it offline after installation. This is useful in facilities or areas with limited or unreliable connectivity.

4. How can I find a topic quickly?

Use the search function to look for relevant clinical topics and information rather than browsing every chapter manually.

5. Who is this app intended for?

It is intended for doctors, nurses, clinical officers, pharmacists and pharmaceutical personnel, medical and health-sciences students, and other healthcare professionals who need a convenient clinical reference.

6. Can I use the app to calculate doses or fluids?

Yes. The app’s dose and fluid calculators can help check calculations and show the related STG section. Always independently verify the inputs and result before making a clinical decision.

7. Is this an official Ministry of Health app?

No. It is independently developed and is not an official publication of, or endorsed by, the Ministry of Health, Tanzania. The guideline content is attributed to its relevant rights holders.

8. Does the app replace clinical judgement or national protocols?

No. It is for educational and clinical reference purposes only. Assess each patient individually, use professional clinical judgement, and follow applicable national and institutional protocols. Confirm important recommendations against the relevant official guideline.

9. Does the app collect personal data?

No. The app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it works offline. Bookmarks and notes are stored only on your device. For further details, read the privacy policy.

10. Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer. An iPhone version may follow.
