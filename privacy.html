Tanzania STG app

1. Introduction

Tanzania STG app (“the App”) is an independently developed Android application designed to provide convenient access to Tanzania's Standard Treatment Guidelines (STG) for clinical reference and educational purposes.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

The App is developed and maintained by Dr. Reward Emmanuel Matata, M.D. (“the Developer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

The App is designed to operate entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share users' personal information.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect or request personal information from users.

The App does not include user accounts, registration, sign-in functionality, advertising services, analytics tools, or tracking technologies.

We do not collect personal identifiers, contact information, location data, device identifiers, usage analytics, or patient health information.

The App is designed to function without transmitting user information to external servers.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide its features, the App may store certain preferences and user-generated content locally on your device, including:

  • Bookmarks: Sections or topics you save for quick access.
  • Personal notes: Notes you create within the App.
  • Recently viewed sections: Information used to help you return to previously accessed content.
  • Display preferences: Settings such as dark mode and text size.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer or third parties by the App.

Locally stored information may be permanently deleted if you clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove its local data. You are responsible for maintaining any copies of information you wish to preserve.

4. Internet Connectivity and Third-Party Services

The App is designed to work without an internet connection.

It does not intentionally transmit personal information to external servers and does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

If future versions introduce online functionality or third-party services that materially change how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Please note that the operating system, app distribution platform, or other services outside the App may process technical information under their own privacy policies.

5. Protection of Patient Information

The App may provide a notes feature for personal reference. However, notes are stored locally and are not encrypted by the App.

Users should not enter patient names, identification numbers, contact details, photographs, or other information that could identify a patient.

Healthcare professionals remain responsible for protecting confidential patient information and complying with applicable professional, ethical, and legal requirements.

The Developer cannot guarantee the confidentiality of locally stored notes if another person gains access to your device or its data.

6. Data Security and Retention

Because the App does not collect personal information, the Developer does not maintain a database of users' personal information.

Information saved by the App remains under the control of the device on which it is stored. Its security may depend on your device's screen lock, operating system, storage protection, and other security settings.

The Developer does not have remote access to, or the ability to recover, locally stored bookmarks, notes, or preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended primarily for healthcare professionals, medical students, and other individuals seeking clinical reference information. It is not specifically directed at children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Third-Party Distribution Platforms

The App may be distributed through platforms such as Google Play or other Android application repositories.

Those platforms may independently collect or process information relating to downloads, installations, updates, or account activity in accordance with their own privacy policies. Such processing is separate from the App's stated offline functionality and is not controlled by the Developer.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality, applicable legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any revised version will be published on the relevant policy page, and the “Last Updated” date will be amended accordingly.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically to remain informed about the App's privacy practices.
